    How to play

    Winning

    Move any piece into your opponent’s den, or capture every enemy piece. A player with no legal move loses. After 100 plies without a capture the game is a draw, and so it is when the same position occurs a third time (the same pieces on the same squares, with the same player to move): two sides shuffling back and forth cannot go on forever.

    Ranks

    Rat 1 · Cat 2 · Dog 3 · Wolf 4 · Leopard 5 · Tiger 6 · Lion 7 · Elephant 8. A piece captures an enemy of equal or lower rank — except that the rat captures the elephant, and the elephant cannot capture the rat.

    Moving

    Each turn, move one piece one square up, down, left or right. You may not enter your own den or a square holding your own piece.

    Rivers

    Only the rat swims. A rat cannot capture across the river bank in either direction, but may capture another rat in the water.

    The lion jumps straight over 2 or 3 water squares, the tiger over exactly 2 — and a rat of either colour on any of those squares blocks the jump.

    Traps

    A piece standing on one of its opponent’s traps has rank zero: any enemy can take it. Your own traps don’t weaken you.

    Controls

    Click a piece to select it (gold ring). Dark dots mark squares it can move to; red rings mark captures. Click a target to move, or another piece to change your selection.

    Undo returns to your previous turn (in AI vs AI, one move while paused). Flip turns the board around. Mute silences the sounds.

    Download Arena .wasm

    The engine and board you are playing are also published as one self-contained WebAssembly Component — the same strategy and the same artwork — for the Jungle Arena.

    1. Download the file below. It is the only file the Arena needs.
    2. Open the Jungle Arena and choose Import .wasm. The Arena checks it and saves it on your device.
    3. Pick it in either seat and start a match. The Arena referees; this program chooses its own moves and draws its own board.
